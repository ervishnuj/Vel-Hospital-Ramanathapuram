About Vel Hospital

Vel Hospital is a private hospital located on Thiruvalluvar Street in the D.D. Vinayagar / Kenikarai area of Ramanathapuram.

It is known locally for ophthalmology (eye care) services and also offers neurological consultations. The hospital is easily accessible near Syed Ammal Matriculation Higher Secondary School.
